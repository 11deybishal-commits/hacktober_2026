VYOM+ — End-to-End AI-Powered GST Invoice Intelligence System
Build an end-to-end invoice intelligence system for VYOM+ that can accept different invoice and transaction document formats, process them using an appropriate AI pipeline, and convert them into accurate, validated, machine-readable financial records.
Input formats
•	Excel (.xlsx)
•	CSV
•	PDF
•	JPEG / JPG
•	PNG
What the system should do
•	Identify the type of input and route it through an appropriate processing pipeline.
•	For Excel/CSV, organize the available information into a clean and structured tabular format.
•	For PDFs and images, extract the relevant invoice and GST information and convert it into structured records.
•	Extract important invoice, GST, tax, financial, and line-item information.
•	Validate extracted information and identify or handle inconsistent or uncertain data where possible.
•	Return standardized machine-readable output such as JSON and/or structured tabular data.
•	Provide a simple interface where an evaluator can upload documents and inspect the resulting structured information.
Main technical focus
The challenge should primarily address reliable document intelligence, with particular attention to improving handwritten GST invoice processing while maintaining strong performance on printed and digitally generated invoices.
AI and technology
•	Teams may use open-source or appropriately licensed OCR systems, vision-language models, document-understanding models, LLMs, and related AI components.
•	The solution should be designed as a complete processing pipeline rather than only a text-recognition demo.
•	The system should be capable of turning real-world invoice documents into accurate and usable financial records.
Expected final outcome
A working document-intelligence system that accepts supported invoice formats and produces validated, structured financial data suitable for downstream accounting workflows.
